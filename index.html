Chrome extension for Google Sheets

See what your formula is looking at.

Click a cell. SheetPeek shows the actual values behind every reference in the formula, with the column header, right next to it. No jumping between sheets.

No explanations. Just the data.

SheetPeek doesn't try to explain your formula. It shows you what the formula is looking at, so you can judge it yourself in one second.

Private by design

  • No account, no sign-in, no OAuth screen
  • No server. Values are read inside your own browser session, from the spreadsheet you already have open
  • Nothing from your spreadsheets is ever sent anywhere. No analytics, no tracking
  • Runs only on docs.google.com/spreadsheets

Works with

VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, SUMIF/SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, QUERY, IFERROR wrappers, whole-column ranges, and cross-sheet references. Anything written in A1 notation.
